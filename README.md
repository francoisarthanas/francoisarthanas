## About Me

I'm **Francois B. Arthanas**, a **Ph.D. Scholar**, **CISSP**, **CISA**, **AAIA**, and Founder of **Cyber Pros**. My work focuses on the intersection of **Agentic AI Governance**, **cybersecurity**, **AI assurance**, **audit readiness**, and **digital trust**.

I explore how organizations can safely design, deploy, monitor, and govern AI systems that are increasingly autonomous, tool-using, and decision-capable. As AI agents begin to reason, plan, call tools, access systems, and act on behalf of humans and organizations, governance must evolve beyond traditional AI policy.

My mission is to help build a future where AI agents are not only powerful, but also **secure, governable, transparent, auditable, and accountable**.

## Agentic AI Governance Focus

Agentic AI introduces a new governance challenge: systems that can reason, plan, use tools, interact with environments, make decisions, and take action with varying levels of autonomy.

My work focuses on practical governance questions such as:

### Identity, Access & Authorization

How should AI agents be identified, authenticated, authorized, and monitored before they interact with enterprise systems?

### Accountability & Auditability

How can organizations trace agent behavior, prompts, decisions, tool calls, approvals, exceptions, and outcomes?

### Human Oversight

What level of human-in-the-loop or human-on-the-loop control is required based on risk, autonomy, and operational impact?

### AI Risk & Compliance

How can AI governance programs align with cybersecurity, privacy, audit, regulatory, and enterprise risk management requirements?

### Secure Deployment

How can organizations deploy agentic systems with proper guardrails, logging, access controls, monitoring, and incident response?
